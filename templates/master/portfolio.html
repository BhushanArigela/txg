Manufacturing & Supply Chain

Enterprise ERP System: Unifying Multi-Department Operations

Operational Challenge

The client relied on fragmented spreadsheets and disconnected tools across purchasing, production, inventory, and finance. This created severe data silos, high error rates in manual entries, and multi-hour delays in generating executive reports.

Engineering Execution

We engineered a centralized Python & React ERP platform with automated REST API data pipelines, connecting every department into a single secure hub with real-time inventory tracking and Power BI dashboards.

Technologies Implemented
Python React PostgreSQL Power BI REST APIs

Quantifiable Business Outcomes

  • ✓ Eliminated redundant manual data entry across departments
  • ✓ Real-time inventory and production floor visibility
  • ✓ Automated executive reporting, reducing decision lead times
  • ✓ Significantly improved operational efficiency and audit control
  • ✓ Scalable foundation ready for multi-location expansion
Impact: Transformed a spreadsheet-reliant operation into a unified real-time enterprise.
Customer Support & Artificial Intelligence

AI Virtual Assistant: Automating Knowledge Retrieval

Operational Challenge

Customer support teams faced a severe backlog due to high volumes of repetitive inquiries, creating long resolution wait times and pulling human agents away from high-priority complex issues.

Engineering Execution

We architected an enterprise AI virtual assistant leveraging Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) with LangChain and ChromaDB vector search to accurately query internal documentation and provide instant support resolutions.

Technologies Implemented
LangChain Haystack ChromaDB Vector Search Commercial LLM APIs

Quantifiable Business Outcomes

  • ✓ Substantial reduction in average ticket resolution time
  • ✓ Automated routine inquiries, freeing support teams for complex cases
  • ✓ 24/7 intelligent customer response availability
  • ✓ Higher customer satisfaction and standardized knowledge delivery
Impact: Scaled support operations without increasing headcount.
AEC & Digital Engineering

BIM SketchUp Automation: Streamlining Complex Structural Workflows

Operational Challenge

Engineering teams were spending significant billable hours performing repetitive manual modeling tasks for ICF (Insulated Concrete Form) block layouts within SketchUp, creating modeling bottlenecks and human calculation errors.

Engineering Execution

We engineered custom SketchUp Ruby plugins that automated 3D block placement algorithms, enforced structural modeling rules, and generated automated bill-of-materials calculations directly inside the CAD interface.

Technologies Implemented
SketchUp Ruby API Ruby on Rails 3D BIM Logic CAD Automation

Quantifiable Business Outcomes

  • ✓ Dramatically accelerated 3D modeling turnaround times
  • ✓ Standardized design accuracy and eliminated manual calculation errors
  • ✓ Freed engineering teams to focus on complex design tasks
  • ✓ Faster client project estimation and delivery schedules
Impact: Automated hours of manual CAD modeling into seconds.
