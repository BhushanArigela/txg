Can I start with just one product?

Absolutely. Each TXG product is designed to operate independently while offering the flexibility to integrate with the rest of the product suite as your needs grow.

Can your products integrate with our existing systems?

Yes. We regularly integrate with accounting software, ERP platforms, CRM systems, HR applications, payment gateways, cloud services, and other third-party solutions.

Do you offer cloud and on-premises deployment?

Yes. Depending on your business and compliance requirements, our products can be deployed in the cloud, on-premises, or in a hybrid environment.

Can the products be customised?

Yes. Every organisation works differently. Our products are designed to be configurable and extensible, allowing us to tailor workflows, reports, dashboards, and integrations to suit your business.
